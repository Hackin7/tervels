Decided to drop by 交通大学 station to see a station, ended up seeing a whole museum building

This museum is about a 交通大学 alumni, Qian Xuesen. In a way he represents what NOC and Exchange is trying to do: (prestigious background) he studied in the uni, went overseas to study at MIT Caltech etc. worked there for a total of 20 years and then came back to China to develop nuclear missiles 🤡

It reminds me a lot of the Toyota museum part on their founder, the idea of Engineering for your country, tho in this case it's clearly more destructive lmao

The museum itself is mainly photos and stuff

I find the story paralleling an average exchange student abroad a lot, trying to juggle learning, travelling and exploring etc.

Overall you can speedrun it in 30min but it's ok, if you drop by the station why not man
