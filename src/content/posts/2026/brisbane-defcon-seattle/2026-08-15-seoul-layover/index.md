2. Then was somehow already at Myeongdong add got Issac toast there lol

3. Went to deoksugung palace and just paid 1000 won to go in bah
    - it's ok spent 1.25h or so
4. Then went to hongdae and spent surprising amount of time at AK mall

5. Walked the street and then took the (surprisingly expensive 17000 won) bus back to airport
