Chamonix was really really fun, talked and yapped to the other exchangers 
(about Polygl0ts and the secret tunnel network? in EPFL).
It was a rather last minute trip, the day before, another exchanger in polygl0ts asked if I wanted to go.

Our rough route was taking a train from Renens VD (Using Friend's Pass + France Train Ticket) to Chamonix
Afterwards we bought a pass (about 99 euros) that included a train up the mountain, and towards the ice cave/ valley
It's super worth wtf, like 1 trip to chamonix with friends pass is like 1 journey up gonergrat + grindelwald combined + cheap France McDonalds

The mountain was really really nice, we took the cable car up a few stops.
It was worth feeling the cold (Especially since the rest of Europe was summer heatwave), 
seeing the mountain with the other exchangers.
I was thinking about going back to some mountains like gonergrat and grindelwald after my exams to see how they are like in summer.
I think with Chamonix I had my fill of that, mountains in Summer are kinda the same, cold (good break from summer weather), with ice, just slightly greener
Or maybe not see how.

After the mountain we took the train to the valley
The valley was really steep and also really nice too.

We didn't manage to see the ice cave though, we reached too late.
We did however walk around the area, see a bit of the ice cave museum which was ok
