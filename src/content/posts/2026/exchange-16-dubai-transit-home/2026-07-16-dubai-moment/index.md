# Dubai moment🙏

At this point I was very very close to exceeding the new 21ksgd budget target i set.

## Airport

They provide SIM Card, but I couldn't get that to work so I just bought and went along.

Ate Burger King which was, ok price for SG standards actually.

Stored baggage for 75 AED, then went off to walk 

# Around Al Fahidi

The new area, the old buildings area all have a vibe to them, along with the skyline

Claude recommendation, was a bit sad got tired to go out even earlier to get Arabic breakfast but this is good enough

The heat is quite rough, if you think Europe or SG is bad, this is desert level, Vegas level

The trick is to offer samples and the shops lure you in they try to buddy buddy too lol

Ended up buying a 8sgd bar of dubai chocolate, not sure if I got scammed, a supermarket sells maybe a worse quality one for 1sgd, Dubai Mall sells for same price, maybe it's a tourist trap thingy
- In hindsight, Dubai Mall has similar prices so maybe not scammed.


# Burj Khalifa

Bit the bullet and went up

The Middle East skyline is quite nice and different from the European ones I've seen, because there's actually tall buildings LOL, and empty desert patches

Feels like a mix of desert old culture and modern (and patches of water instead of river streams)

The building itself is ok, tallest in the world

# Food 

1. Wanted to find UAE food
2. Realised all exceeded budget
3. Settled on Lebanese food which should be close?
4. Walked past some restaurant with 75aed meal and just went for it

A lot of native food looked similar to Indian or kebab or other kinds so very hard to distinguish

Anyway the food is... Kinda tastes similar to how it looks while the bread thingy is a nice touch. Not bad, but tasted before
