# Japan Expo Paris - Overall

Some thoughts about Japan Expo Paris (editing) 

Location
1. It's about 4 big halls, which is decently sized
   - a lot of stages like Dokomi
2. Unlike most other conventions the Creators Area (and a fair bit of areas) is intentionally broken up and split between the areas
    - I kinda like it this way because it encourages more exploration throughout the venue
    - might be tiring though lol
3. It doesn't have the breadth and depth of Dokomi (Dokomi is like 2x)
   - no random bouncy castles or itasha for example

Unique Booths
1. Manga booths, and an "act out the manga" sections. A LOT of them (yomi no tsugai get to shoot daemons, witch hat atelier get to wear hat do magic, etc.)
2. Papercrafts, Paris Music Game community, their other jap community booths too
3. Traditional Japanese section booths? Like Creators Area but just for traditional Japanese stuff idk
4. Ankama - I'm still not too sure (some French multimedia franchise) what it is but ok

Like dokomi it has the traditional Japanese stuff, Japanese tourism, though less

**Unique Creators stuff**
1. I noticed a LOT of terrariums, flower vases, 3d figurines and stuff like that
    - the amount of terrariums is a LOT. 
    - not just regular figurines but like, clay figurines, beads, etc. 
2. I really really like the idea of stamp rallys
  - they have that for various franchises, like jojos, vtuber, umamusumes
  - it acts as both a good map to find booths with franchises you like, and a good way to justify your spending🤡 (fr tho the value is worth for Europe) 
3. They had so much dream journey lol (uma) 
  - also witch hat etc. 

There's more of the standard creator stuff, I've been skimming through it more nowadays. Creators have been though both dokomi, polymanga and this so bout there

Performances
1. As usual there were musical performances, Yoshiki was quite interesting
2. The Sakura stage had traditional Japanese art form performances, by both Japanese people and the local groups (Taikoyaki), that was quite fun
3. French convention so main language french🇫🇷 
4. There was a project Sekai panel with 2 of the more more jump VAs
    - got some qns answering, then Live reading by the VAs which was quite cool
    - then they showed songs from the movie with the 3d models for 20min lol. It was full version at least so got some vibes
    - then the VAs did like karaoke of the more more jump songs with us lol 
5. There was an attack on titan cosplay show, where cosplayers re-enact attack on titan, like wtf

Food
1. Meh
2. The Ice cream bubble waffle was good but it's 12e lol
3. I smuggled in KFC for much cheaper
    - France KFC is cheap and good, like 5e meal with sundae? 
4. Onigiri is ok, got 2 for 4.80e (they were clearing, it was supposed to be 1)
5. There was a convenience store inside the convention, that was interesting, the muffin is like 3e tho lol

Others

I feel like I could do it in 1 day and not 2, then again the 2nd day gave me time to chill and watch more performances (and I did enjoy more), so definitely 2 days is a sweet spot to chill
