Asian supermarkets!!!

I haven't found them in Swiss ngl so seeing haidilao was an experience

The Chinese one had Chinese stuff, Japan one had Japan stuff, really felt like those countries lol

Also the ToGo meals are quite cheap (7€) so just got one for dinner

More traditional manga shop (Manga Mafia) with some rather niche (shonen jump cancelled) manga ngl

Little Tokyo is a very vibey place, the stalls are not particularly amazing tbh, similar to Lausanne's Mix Café (in fact smaller), but there's a certain small charm to
1. there being quite a few of them,
2. Catering to both German and the Japanese audience (not just selling but trying to serve the Japanese people with Japan books and Japan Groceries)
3. Japanese people actually being there, and the experience actually does feel a bit like tokyo, quite authentic
