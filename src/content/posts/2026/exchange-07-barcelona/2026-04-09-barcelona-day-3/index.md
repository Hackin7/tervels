Just when we thought it's the end there's still the whole museum about it, which I think is good, understand more about history

The Sagrada Familia ngl is just Gaudi make such a good outline that the project still continues on, even to this day (it's not fully completed). Mans design requires advances in tech to work lmao

It takes a lot of inspiration from Nature (they keep saying the columns is from trees), geometry, crystals

The statues each have a reason behind their design, glass colour staining etc. Let more light in etc.

The interactive visualisations are also quite nice, projection mapping onto models of the buildings etc.
