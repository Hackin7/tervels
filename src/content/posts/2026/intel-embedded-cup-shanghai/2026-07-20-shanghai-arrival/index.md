## Shanghai D2: Appraisal Day 1

Afterwards it's just testing and testing and testing

For context our project is a visual assistant for the blind
The whole reason for this trip is for the Intel Embedded Cup, its a contest where you are given an Intel Board with an NPU, and you make a project with it and present

We made it able to
1. Speech to Text
2. Describe a scene
3. Pre-emptively identify hazards

We have a dual LLM Architecture with Qwen 3.5 and Gemma, Qwen 3.5 always does scene description, Gemma answers the questions of the users

Last min we were evaluating models and settled on Gemma 4 VLM for answering questions
