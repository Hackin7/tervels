# Brighton

The Smoked Mackerel is quite decent

The Fish and Chips have nice breading but kinda plain, but I think with enough salt and seasoning it's kinda nice
Quite interesting to learn that in UK you have to add however much seasoning you want and not have it pre cooked

# Seven Sisters

We took a bus to Seven Sisters' cliff from Brighton

Very nice place, kinda reminds me of Monts Klint with the white, just without the chalking stones
