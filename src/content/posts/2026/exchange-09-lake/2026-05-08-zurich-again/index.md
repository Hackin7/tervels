# University of Zurich UZH 

New trip to Zurich new (this time unintentional) trip to a Zurich University

This time University of Zurich UZH

I was walking by the lake and saw that a botanic garden was nearby and when I reached I realised it belonged to UZH

At first I thought it's a typical park, but there's a LOT of different plant species


And the terrariums stood out to me

### Terrarium 1 

I think the weather inside is more similar to outside but slightly more humid

### Terrarium 2
