Isao Takahata

Didn't know the guy invented layouts
Very nice museum, got the cell artwork and stuff also

I find it quite interesting that he can speak French and got inspired by French animation lol, and that influenced his earlier works, he travelled to swiss to learn about the mountain life and make anime on it lol. Back then hayao miyazaki was with him as a layout artist also lol

Afterwards he made more Japanese related anime, cello and WW2 stories, and experimented with animation

He also did the bamboo shoot princess kaguya movie, didn't know that he invented new digital techniques for colouring to allow for the line art and make that work

I really liked seeing the cells and the drawings (the princess kaguya one is quite unique, cutout paper instead of cells coloured)
