1. Use MPI in a very basic way
2. Optimise step by step into various subtasks
It's very useful as an exchange student who just wants to learn the content and not do CP lmao

while CS3210 in NUS is really they just throw you a problem and you solve it
but I feel like you might really get good by solving the hard problems, which is also why I dont really feel like mapping the EPFL mod to NUS, where's the challenge and fun 🤡
