# BMW Museum

Things of BMW Museums
1. BMW started off by making airplane engines, then WW1 happened and they pivoted to motorbikes and cars
2. Then Nazis made them make jet engines again and use forced labour
3. I didn't really see any technical deep dive, mainly going through the various cars and motorbikes and engines
4. They emphasise on art a lot
   - sketches
   - temporary exhibition about the Italian designers involved
    - art cars - painting on the cars
5. The future concepts look quite nice, futuristic minimalistic

It's nice to contrast this to the Toyota museum in Nagoya, there was quite inspiring and a lot to see, technical deep dives into the technology (especially car safety, MUSIC algo which I really liked, founders motivations etc. ) 
Tbh this has less of that and is more artsy, doesn't really explain all the considerations behind the art but nice to look at

BMW Welt is Free and like a Car show floor, not bad

English Garden has a Chinese Pagoda, the lakes look interesting too
